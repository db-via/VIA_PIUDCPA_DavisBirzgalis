# E2E Tests

# Test 1: View and Navigate Celestial Body Information

# Precondition
The user opens `index.html`.

# Steps
1. Click on Kerbin.
2. Verify that the information panel opens.
3. Verify that the panel displays Kerbin.
4. Click Next.
5. Verify that the next celestial body is displayed.
6. Press Escape.
7. Verify that the information panel closes.

# Expected Result
The user can open celestial body information, navigate between bodies and close the information panel without errors.
